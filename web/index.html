Customs: see which early holders of a NEAR memecoin were funded by its creator

Pick a NEP-141 token account. The page reads the chain live from FastNEAR, lists the first accounts that received the token, and flags any account whose NEAR funding traces back to the creator within 3 hops. Nothing on this page is stored or pre-computed.

1. Chain connection

What you are seeing: the latest block the RPC node reports. Every balance below is read at this block.

Loading

2. Token and creator

What you are seeing: the token's metadata and the first transaction that touched the token account, whose signer is taken as the creator.

Waiting

3. Early holders

What you are seeing: the first accounts that received the token, in order, found in the token account's first transactions. Balance is what each holds now, read at the block above.

Waiting

4. Funded by the creator within 3 hops

What you are seeing: for each early holder, a search backward through plain NEAR transfers received before its first token receipt. A flag means a printed chain of transfers from the creator, each linked to its transaction.

Waiting
Limits, stated plainly: only direct NEAR transfers are followed (not transfers sent by contracts, not Intents routing), accounts with over 3000 transactions are not expanded, and the search window is bounded. No flag does not prove a holder is unrelated to the creator. The full funding rule is in the repository README.
